Group

Group Experiences

Humans have always gathered for ritual, movement, celebration, grief, initiation and transformation.

A group brings relationship, witnessing and collective energy. It can also reveal parts of us that only emerge when other people are in the room. My current group work moves between intensity and stillness:

Breath of Becoming

●

Suggested imageBreathwork space, mid-session · 3:2

A holotropic-inspired breathwork initiation that gives the body space and permission to feel, express, release, and move through what may not be accessible in ordinary life.

Conscious Dance Journeys

●

Suggested imageGroup in movement, motion-blurred · 3:2

A primal, non-verbal journey through movement, feeling, connection, and celebration, returning again to stillness.

Listening Temples

●

Suggested imageListening circle, quiet light · 3:2

Sharing circles devoted to listening as a sacred practice.

A space to become quieter, receive another person fully, and experience what becomes possible when listening is given more importance than speaking.

Experience Design & Curation

Especially when people are being invited into vulnerability, intensity, or their edges, the quality of the container matters.

Thoughtful design helps create spaces that are safe enough to go deep, coherent enough to trust, and strong enough to hold what unfolds.

Collaboration & Guest Facilitation

If there is resonance in the intention, values, and way of working, I’m open to creating high-quality, high-integrity transformational and healing experiences together.
