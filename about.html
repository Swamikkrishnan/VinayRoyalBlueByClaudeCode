About

My Path

I did not plan to become a healer.

I was living a very ordinary life — work, achievement, relationships, the usual momentum — when a door opened that I had not gone looking for.

And once it opened, I couldn’t close it again.

A door opened

It started with a single, direct experience of my own nervous system — something I could not explain away, and could not un-know once I’d felt it.

What followed was years of immersion: trainings, practices, teachers, my own healing, and a slow rebuilding of how I understood being human.

The body had been trying to tell me something for years. I just hadn’t learned how to listen.

Then ordinary life took the work further

Marriage, parenting, running a household, holding responsibility for people who depended on me — these ordinary demands became as much a part of my training as any workshop.

They asked things of me that no seminar could: patience under fatigue, presence under conflict, staying open when it would have been easier to close.

The challenge

Real relationships don’t pause for your practice. They ask you to meet them exactly as they are, in the moment they are asking.

The capacity it built

A steadiness that doesn’t depend on ideal conditions — the same steadiness I bring into a session with you.

Today, blind spots continue to reveal themselves

The work has not stopped and I don’t expect it to. But something has settled that lets me hold much more than I used to be able to.

Deep rest
A baseline of rest that doesn’t depend on nothing going wrong.
Peace
A peace that can hold difficulty without needing to fix or flee it.
Ordinary rhythms
Sleep, appetite, and attention that return to their own natural rhythm.

Life invigorates me more than it drains me now. That shift is the whole point of the work.

The Dream

I dream of a world where the people holding power — over money, decisions, relationships, or influence — are asking three questions before they act:

  1. 1

    What does this serve?

  2. 2

    Whom will it affect?

  3. 3

    What will it cost?

I envision a world where power serves life.
