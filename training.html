The Path

My healing and transformation began in June 2016, together with my training as a practitioner.

Since then, over 3,000 hours across more than 200 workshops, trainings, seminars, retreats, resits and assisting roles — including work with more than 1,000 people through paid and gift sessions, supervised work, peer exchange and training settings.

Certified Qualifications

Trainings completed and certified

Craniosacral Therapy (CST)

A non-invasive process that works through gentle touch to release stress, disturbance, injury and deep-seated holding.

Holotropic Breathing Technique

A practice using rapid, deep breathing and music to support non-ordinary states of consciousness for emotional process.

NLP Practitioner & Breakthrough Coach

Foundational training in neuro-linguistic programming and breakthrough coaching methods.

Access Bars Practitioner

Extensive Practice & Study

Pathways worked with at length

The Journey

642 hours · 2017–2020 · full pathway completed twice

A specialised emotional healing framework designed to access and resolve deep-seated trauma or suppressed emotions. Developed by Brandon Bays in the 1990s, it operates on the philosophy that unresolved emotional pain is held in the body and must be consciously met.

EFT & Matrix Reimprinting

195+ hours · 2016–2020 · advanced levels, repeat study and assisting

Combines gentle tapping on acupressure points with guided conversation to release intense emotion and revisit the memories behind it.

Note: final accreditation for The Journey and EFT required additional case-study documentation, which I did not complete.
