1:1 Offerings

Healing simplified

Healing is our natural capacity to regenerate, adapt and grow.

When something gets stuck, this can help create movement.

A healer works with this process through what they have embodied, alongside tools, skills and interventions.

A healer does not heal you. They help you access and expand your own capacity to heal.

When we work together

The work is a collaboration. I bring care, skill and discernment, while staying attentive to your pace, boundaries and what is changing as we go.

You remain in charge of your life and your choices.

What helps the work go deeper:

Honesty
Say what is true, including doubt, resistance, disagreement and what is hard to name.
Responsibility
Take ownership of your choices, needs, boundaries and impact.
Communication
Speak up when something feels off. Give notice when plans change. Address misunderstandings early.
Commitment
Keep returning to what you chose to work on, especially when it becomes difficult.
Action
Let inner shifts become lived changes: a conversation, a boundary, a decision, a period of rest, a new habit, a practical step.

You do not need to do any of this perfectly. These are values we keep working on.

90-Day Crucible

Death · Rebirth · Integration

For
A deeper commitment to your path, with greater responsibility and accountability over three concentrated months.
The intention
To create the conditions for significant growth, healing and transformation in a shorter period of time.
What the Crucible asks and offers↓

The Crucible is forged around a death and rebirth cycle:

  1. What has completed?
  2. What needs to end?
  3. What future version of you is asking to be birthed?

Alongside whatever intention you enter with, we build your capacity to notice when something is off, respond, repair, integrate and find movement again.

Your real contribution is the ferocity of your commitment to the container and to yourself.

Scope & Suitability

What this work is, and is not

This work can support healing, self-understanding, emotional processing, embodiment, clarity, behaviour change and personal growth.

It does not replace medical care, psychiatric treatment, psychotherapy, emergency support or other licensed professional services where those are needed. If you are in crisis or need clinical care, please reach a qualified medical professional or local emergency services first.

The form and Alignment Call help us assess whether what I offer is appropriate for your situation. If it is not, I will say so clearly and, where possible, point you toward a more suitable kind of support.
