When We Work Together

What the relationship asks

The work is a collaboration. You remain in charge of your life and your choices; my role is to bring everything I can in service of what is ready to move. These are values we both agree to be accountable to — not to embody perfectly all the time.

From my side

  • AttunementI pay attention to you, your pace and what is changing as we go. I may guide the process, while continuing to listen and adjust.
  • CareI treat your trust as something to earn and protect. Your dignity, privacy, safety and wellbeing guide how I work with you.
  • IntegrityI hold myself to the standards I ask of you. I continue my own practice, respect my limits, keep my word and repair when I fall short.
  • TrustI trust your capacity, even when you cannot feel it. I can guide, challenge and steady you. You remain in charge of your process.

From your side

  • HonestySay what you notice as plainly as you can. This includes doubt, resistance, disagreement and what feels difficult to name.
  • ResponsibilityStay in charge of your process and your choices. Notice when you hand that authority away, and practise stepping back into it.
  • BoundariesLet your yes, no, slower, not yet and I need something different have a voice. Clear boundaries allow trust and depth to grow together.
  • CommitmentDeeper work can bring up resistance, avoidance and convincing reasons to pull away. Commitment means continuing to show up and return to the work you chose, especially when it becomes difficult.

Between us

Communication

We stay in communication with each other. We respond, give notice when plans need to change, speak up when something feels off, and address misunderstandings rather than letting them quietly accumulate.

For ongoing work, this also means being reasonably responsive between sessions and treating agreed times and commitments with respect.

What the Work Asks of You

The work begins with willingness.

Self-responsibility

Take ownership of your choices, actions, inactions, needs, boundaries and impact.

When hurt, trauma or abuse is part of the story, see clearly what happened, what was not okay and where you had no power. Then look honestly at what is yours: where you overrode yourself, handed away authority, repeated a pattern or still have something to learn.

The movement is from victim to victor: acknowledging what happened while reclaiming the power to decide what happens next.

Stay with what is difficult

Meet what has been avoided and stay long enough to see what is really there.

Some things are wisely avoided because they are genuinely not good for us. Others sit at the edge of healing, growth, truth or a greater capacity we have not yet learned to hold. Find out which is which.

Act

Translate inner shifts into outer ones: a conversation · a boundary · a change in food or sleep · a period of rest · a new habit · a difficult decision · a practical step.

Even when it is boring. Even when self-sabotage shows up. Even when the environment does not support you. Even when you fall off and have to begin again. Keep following through.

Single Sessions · Paid in advance

Five doorways

Each session is shaped around a particular need rather than a fixed procedure. What any one draws on depends on the person and on what emerges.

Introductory pricing: I want this work to remain accessible while I build the practice. Rates will increase gradually as my schedule fills.

Body

Rest & Reconnection

90 minutes · ₹1,000

For when your system needs space to settle, unwind, release and reconnect — leaving you lighter, clearer, more energised, and more able to hear your body, feelings and thoughts.

A typical session may include optional smudging, an opening mantra, space to speak, osteopathic techniques, fascial massage, craniosacral touch and Access Bars. The structure is flexible and responds to what your body needs to settle, release and integrate.

Mind

Clarity & Confidence

60 minutes · ₹750

For mental turmoil, confusion, dilemmas, limiting beliefs, or needing to see your situation more clearly. We untangle what is happening, widen perspective, and help you arrive at a clearer understanding of yourself, your options, and what matters most.

Draws from NLP, Breakthrough Coaching, guided inquiry and perspective work, alongside my own way of seeing and reflecting patterns.

Emotion

Peace & Freedom

2–4 hours · ₹2,500

For working with what sits beneath the surface and continues to shape the present. The process can open access to buried emotion, memory and meaning, creating more peace with what has been and more freedom in what comes next.

Sessions may draw from The Journey, EFT, inner child work, conscious connected breathing and other trauma-informed emotional processing techniques, depending on what emerges. These sessions require prior screening and are used selectively, with enough space for preparation and integration.

Action

Momentum & Motivation

1 × 60-minute session + 4 × 15-minute follow-ups · ₹1,500

For when something you care about needs to become real. We work with what is creating friction, build practical structure, and support accountability and follow-through so you can finally begin, continue and keep moving.

The process centres around creating a clear action plan, then using short follow-up calls to troubleshoot resistance, maintain momentum and keep the plan alive in real life.

Breath

Range & Regulation

60 minutes · ₹750

For developing a more conscious relationship with the breath and expanding the range of states you can access and move through. Depending on what is needed, the breath may support regulation, focus, energy, emotional movement or a deeper intervention.

Sessions may include breath education, pranayama, regulation practices, activation, movement, sound and conscious connected breathing, shaped around what is most useful for you.

The exchange for each session is discussed on the Alignment Call, so it can be matched to what you actually need rather than to a list.

90-Day Crucible

Death–rebirth cycle

Donation-based

For a deeper commitment to your path and a greater level of responsibility and accountability with life. The intention is radical timeline compression — imagine accessing three years of growth, healing and transformation within three concentrated months.

What the Crucible asks and offers

The Crucible is forged around a death-rebirth cycle:

What has completed? What needs to end? What future version of you is asking to be birthed?

Alongside whatever intention you enter the Crucible for, we deliberately install a capacity for self-restoration: noticing when something is off, responding, repairing, integrating and finding movement again.

Your real contribution is the ferocity of your commitment to the container and to yourself.

Scope & Suitability

What this work is, and is not

This work can support healing, self-understanding, emotional processing, embodiment, clarity, behaviour change and personal growth.

It does not replace medical care, psychiatric treatment, psychotherapy, emergency support or other licensed professional services where those are needed. If you are in crisis or need clinical care, please reach a qualified medical professional or local emergency services first.

The form and Alignment Call help us assess whether what I offer is appropriate for your situation. If it is not, I will say so clearly and, where possible, point you toward a more suitable kind of support.
