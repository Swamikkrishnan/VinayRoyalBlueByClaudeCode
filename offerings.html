When we work together.

I care deeply about your healing, freedom, peace, happiness, success, personal power and the grounded confidence to live as fully yourself.

If we choose to work together, this is what the relationship asks of both of us.

Single Sessions

Five doorways

Each session is shaped around a particular need rather than a fixed procedure. What any one draws on depends on the person and on what emerges.

Body

Rest & Reconnection

90 minutes

For when your system needs space to settle, unwind, release and reconnect. Sessions may include optional smudging, osteopathic techniques, fascial massage, craniosacral touch and Access Bars.

Mind

Clarity & Confidence

60 minutes

For mental turmoil, confusion, or dilemmas. Draws from NLP, Breakthrough Coaching, guided inquiry and perspective work.

Emotion

Peace & Freedom

2–4 hours

For working with what sits beneath the surface. Sessions may draw from The Journey, EFT, inner child work, and trauma-informed techniques. Requires a prior conversation.

Action

Momentum & Motivation

1 × 60-minute session + 4 × 15-minute follow-ups

For when something you care about needs to become real. Centres around creating a clear action plan with short follow-up calls to maintain momentum.

Breath

Range & Regulation

60 minutes

Sessions may include breath education, pranayama, regulation practices, activation, movement, sound and conscious connected breathing.

The exchange for each session is discussed on the Alignment Call, so it can be matched to what you actually need rather than to a list.

Longer support

30-Day Intensive

A tailor-made period of focused work, with continuity between sessions.

For a period when one area of life needs sustained attention — helping a new movement take root, or staying with one issue long enough for something significant to unwind, resolve or change.

Between sessions you can message by text or voice note of up to 2.5 minutes, and I respond within 24 hours.

Availability & scope

Practical details

Sessions are available in person in Auroville and online, Monday to Saturday at 11:00 AM. Alignment Calls are at 3:00 PM or 3:45 PM.

This work does not replace medical care, psychiatric treatment or psychotherapy. If you are in crisis or need clinical care, please reach a qualified medical professional or local emergency services first.

You do not need to know which session you need. We work that out together.
