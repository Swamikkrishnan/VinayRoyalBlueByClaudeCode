How the work takes shape

Where life is stuck, we create movement.

Where life is expanding, we build capacity.

The same life situation can show up in different ways within us.

  • in the body
  • in how we think
  • in what we feel
  • in the way we breathe
  • in what we do or avoid doing

Sometimes we begin with what is loudest.
Sometimes with what has been ignored.
