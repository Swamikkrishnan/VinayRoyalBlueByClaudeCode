My name is Vinay Swaminathan.

I’m a healer and facilitator living in Auroville, building a life around nature, community and conscious living.

My work is rooted in emotional range, embodied awareness and the capacity to meet more of life with greater freedom.

What matters to me is how this is lived in ordinary life.

Where we begin

Where life is stuck, we create movement.

Where life is expanding, we build capacity.

A challenge may need attention through:

  • Body
  • Mind
  • Emotion
  • Breath
  • Action

Sometimes we begin with what is loudest.
Sometimes with what has been ignored.
