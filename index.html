Why welcome all of life?

When we feel welcome, we feel wanted and loved. We know that we belong.

We relax. We open up. It becomes easier to connect and contribute.

What if we offered life the same welcome?

What we welcome, we can come closer to and understand better.

As we welcome life more wholeheartedly, it reveals more of itself:

  • depth
  • beauty
  • intensity
  • stillness
  • mystery

It becomes less something to fear, tolerate or just get through, and more something to enter, experience and enjoy.

Welcoming all of life is more than an idea. It is a lifelong practice.

Over time, this practice can become tangible in greater vitality, healthy immune and hormonal function, and a growing capacity for peace, warmth, care and connection. It can also look like a nervous system becoming more resilient and antifragile — less easily thrown off balance, quicker to recover and increasingly able to grow stronger through challenge.

Five doorways into the work

Are you finding yourself at the edge of your current capacity?

Whether you feel deeply stuck and restricted, or you are actively expanding and need an upgrade to hold what comes next — life is asking for a new kind of movement.

Sometimes that movement requires rest. Sometimes perspective, emotional completion, committed action, or a different relationship with the breath.
