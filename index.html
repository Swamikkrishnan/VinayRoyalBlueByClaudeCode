My name is Vinay Swaminathan.

I’m a healer and facilitator living in Auroville, building a life around nature, community, creativity and conscious ways of living.

At the heart of my work is developing a deeper relationship with our emotions: greater range, capacity, intelligence, maturity and responsibility.

What matters is how this shows up in ordinary life. How we care for our bodies, relate to people, work, handle money, make choices, keep our word and live with a clear conscience.
